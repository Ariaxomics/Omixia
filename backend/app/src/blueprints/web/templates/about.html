About Omixia

Omixia is designed to support molecular pathologists and geneticists in the interpretation and reporting of somatic cancer variants.

The platform integrates:

  • SNV, CNV and SV analysis
  • Context-aware tiering
  • Audit-tracked interpretation
  • Config-driven reporting
  • Reproducible clinical documentation
